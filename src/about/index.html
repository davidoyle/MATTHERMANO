Matt Hermano

I am a Vancouver-based singer-songwriter writing intimate songs about longing, quiet courage, and the small moments that stay with us.

Most nights you can find me in cafes and open mics around the city, refining songs in front of rooms that reward attention and honesty.

My sets are designed to feel conversational: minimal production, lyrical focus, and a strong connection with the audience.

My music journey

  1. Age 13

    The first guitar

    My music journey started at the age of 13 when my dad gifted me my very first guitar. Starting off I was very interested in flamenco and Latin music. Over time I got into other genres and developed a love and respect for all types of music.

  2. Age 16

    First songs, first stage

    By 16 I started to compose and write songs. My first ever performance was for a multicultural day in high school. It sparked the fire of wanting to perform in front of people.

  3. Ages 18–21

    Competitions

    From 18 to 21 I entered battle of the bands and Christian songwriting competitions.

  4. Age 22

    Learning to sing

    At 22 I took vocal lessons and learned how to sing properly.

  5. Age 24

    Putting it down

    At 24 I gave up music and my dreams for the person I loved, thinking it was the right thing to do.

  6. Age 28

    Music as therapy

    By 28 the relationship had ended, and I returned to music as therapy.

  7. Age 29

    Three silvers for Canada

    At 29 I represented Canada in a World Competition of Performing Arts, in songwriting, pop and R&B, and won three silver medals.

    After the competition I didn't pursue music as a career. I felt I was not good enough, and I still carried trauma from the previous relationship about my dream of becoming a singer-songwriter and performer.

  8. Age 36

    Going after it

    Fast forward to 36. I found a relationship where my partner pushes me to perform, write songs, and pursue the dream of becoming a full-time songwriter and performer.

Hear the story live.

Matt plays house concerts, listening rooms, venue sets and private events. Based in Vancouver, BC.

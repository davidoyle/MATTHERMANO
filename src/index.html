About

Soul that came back the long way

Matt Hermano is a Vancouver singer-songwriter whose sound is rooted in 70s Motown and 2000s pop. He picked up his first guitar at 13, won three silver medals for Canada at 29, and walked away from music twice before it pulled him back. Now he writes about longing and quiet courage, and sings to rooms that listen.
